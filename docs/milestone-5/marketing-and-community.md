## Objectives

- promote the plugin to relevant WordPress, Cardano/NMKR, NFT, and integration audiences;
- launch across at least two appropriate channels;
- drive measurable, real adoption toward the contractual download/install/visit targets;
- conduct at least one useful community demo/Q&A with a target of at least 10 actual attendees;
- create a route for early adopters to provide detailed feedback and receive support.

## Core message

Initial message direction, subject to release/readiness decisions:

- connect NMKR Cardano/Solana NFT data to WordPress;
- synchronize data locally and render through plugin shortcodes/interfaces;
- explain the supported free feature set accurately;
- point users to transparent open-source documentation, security/testing evidence, and troubleshooting material;
- primary CTA: install the current supported release, configure NMKR access, synchronize, and render the first supported free view;
- secondary CTA: join the community demo/Q&A and provide feedback.

Do not market Premium capabilities, WordPress.org availability, compatibility, security assurance, or adoption figures beyond what the actual release/evidence supports.

## Planned channels

At least two channels are required. Candidate mix:

1. project/ROCSI website and GitHub Release;
2. ROCSI social channels such as LinkedIn/X where available;
3. relevant Catalyst/Cardano/NMKR communities where promotion is permitted;
4. relevant WordPress developer/integration communities where self-promotion follows local rules;
5. targeted, personalized outreach to relevant developers/operators/agencies with a credible use case.

Final channel selection, URLs, timestamps, and analytics belong in the execution log.

## Targeted outreach

Prefer a small high-relevance list over scraped or bulk unsolicited messaging. Candidate audiences include WordPress developers working with blockchain/NFT projects, NMKR/Cardano operators with public WordPress sites, and agencies with relevant integration clients.

The initial planning range is roughly 20-40 relevant contacts, adjusted based on available legitimate public relationships and channel rules. This is a planning number, not a contractual target.

## Community event

Preferred first event: approximately 45 minutes, e.g. 25 minutes product/install/demo and 20 minutes Q&A.

Operational goal: recruit enough registrations/interest to make >=10 actual attendees realistic. A second office-hours/Q&A session is a contingency, not a requirement if the first event satisfies the need.

Record event date/platform, public announcement, registration count where privacy-safe, actual distinct attendance count, topics/questions, and follow-up route.
