# Project Completion Report and Project Close-out Video

**Status:** NOT READY FOR FINAL DRAFT\
**Document type:** Close-out requirements/gates and eventual submission record

Do not prepare a final PCR narrative or record the final PCV before the material Milestone 5 outcomes, final figures, release state, support/feedback results, post-launch adjustment disposition, and final repository provenance are known.

## PCR planning checklist

Current close-out guidance must be re-verified immediately before submission. The planning expectation is a concise report covering, at minimum:

- project name and Catalyst proposal/IdeaScale reference;
- project/proposal number and project manager;
- start/completion dates;
- challenge/project KPIs;
- key achievements, especially collaboration/community engagement;
- measurable impact and adoption results;
- why the project matters;
- technical/public source and supporting evidence links;
- public PCV link;
- truthful disclosure of unmet, partially met, or externally pending items.

Final length/portal-field requirements must come from current Catalyst guidance at submission time rather than this scaffold alone.

## PCV planning checklist

Current guidance must also be re-verified before production. Initial planning expectation:

- public video hosted on an accepted public platform;
- roughly 2-5 minutes unless current rules change;
- suitable resolution/quality;
- technical demo/walkthrough for this technical project;
- challenge, approach, technical solution, and funding context;
- progression, milestones/KPIs, learning, challenges, and achieved/not-achieved results;
- demonstration of delivered outputs;
- what comes next, sustainability/commercialization/funding direction where relevant;
- innovation/ecosystem value and why the project matters.

Captions are recommended for accessibility/quality but should not be labelled mandatory unless current Catalyst guidance says so.

## Evidence prerequisites before final drafting

- final release/version and public source SHA/tree;
- GitHub Release and actual WordPress.org submission/listing status;
- download/active-install/unique-visit results with methodology;
- community event attendance evidence;
- detailed feedback from distinct users;
- support summary;
- post-launch adjustment and observed result, or documented unresolved contingency;
- final handover review;
- reconciled evidence register and traceability matrix.

## Preparation gate

`READY FOR DRAFT` requires M5-08 to have reconciled the above evidence. Drafting may begin earlier only for neutral structure/boilerplate, not final claims or figures.
